Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and the financial transactions you choose to log. We also collect basic technical information like your IP address and browser type to ensure the service functions correctly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. We use your information to maintain your account, calculate your spending totals, and categorize your transactions.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your sensitive financial information from unauthorized access.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your financial data to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party infrastructure providers (like hosting services), who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
